# Loop 4: mobile export (build spec)

Branch `v0.2-mobile-export`, on top of loops 1 to 3 (branch `v0.2-tracks-layouts`, pull request #2 to main). A recording can also be rendered as a vertical 9:16 file for phones, made on request in the edit step from the same originals; the share page serves it to portrait phones and offers a switch; both files can be downloaded. Plan decision (BUILD_PLAN_v0.2.md): a second rendered file, not composing on the share page.

## What changes for the user

- The edit step's tools gain a **Phone version** toggle (a `.tg` toggle like step 1's, off by default) at the end of the Layout row's second line, under the strip. When it is on:
  - A **region box** appears over the preview's screen picture: the part of the screen the phone version keeps. It is 16:9 by default; a **Shape** pair next to the toggle offers **Wide** (16:9) and **Square** (1:1). Drag it to place it; a size strip (Half, Two thirds, Whole, of the screen's width) sets its size; arrow keys nudge it, as the bubble's do. It never leaves the screen picture. The box is drawn as a 3px vermilion outline with the outside dimmed to 45%, so the kept part reads as the picture and the rest as discarded.
  - A small **phone preview** plate (9:16, 160px tall) beside the thumbnail shows the vertical frame as it will render (region on top, camera below), refreshed like the thumbnail; it is the phone version's poster.
  - Upload and get link uploads both files; Download MP4 gains **Download phone MP4** beside it. The progress text says "Rendering the phone version..." for the second render.
- The phone frame is 1080x1920 (capped like the others; a smaller screen is scaled up, which is what a region does). With a camera: the region cover-cropped into the top band (1080x608 for Wide, 1080x1080 for Square), the camera cover-cropped into the band below, a 2px black rule between, black background. Without a camera: the region in the same top band and the rest of the frame black; with Padded chosen, the padded colour instead of black. The bubble is not drawn in the phone version.
- The share page serves the phone version to a portrait phone (viewport width at most 900px and taller than wide, decided at load) and the landscape file otherwise, with a **Phone / Full** switch (two `.act--quiet` squares under the player) whenever both exist; the choice is remembered for the page in sessionStorage. Download there offers both. Open Graph tags keep the landscape file and thumbnail.
- The library row's size cell says "Phone version" under the size (like "Originals kept"). Change layout (again mode) comes back with the phone version's settings and can add, change or drop it; a new phone render replaces the old file under the same link.
- Nothing changes while recording.

## Rendering (`public/layouts.js`, `public/render.js`)

`layouts.js` gains a `mobile(region, variant, screenW, screenH, hasCamera, colour)` geometry: `{W: 1080, H: 1920, screen: {x, y, w, h, fit: 'cover', region: {x, y, w, h}}, camera: {...} | null, background}` where `region` is the kept part of the screen as shares (x, y, w, h of the screen picture, w and h from the shape and size). `fitRect` learns to take a source region. `render.js` gains the `mobile` draw and `renderLayout` takes `{layout: 'mobile', region, variant}`; `grabLayout` the same for the phone poster. The phone version is rendered after the landscape file, with the same trim and cuts and the same audio. Performance: report the phone render's speed in Details beside the layout's; no new target (it is a second full render).

## Edit step (`public/editor.js`, `public/index.html`, `public/app.css`, new `public/region.js`)

- `region.js` owns the region box: the stored region (`{x, y, size: 'half'|'twothirds'|'whole', shape: 'wide'|'square'}`, default centred, two thirds, wide), its view over `#clipFrame` (drag, arrow keys, sizes, shapes), clamping to the screen picture's box (which `previewVars` already gives), and `onChange`. Keep it under 250 lines; borrow the pointer and keyboard pattern from `bubble.js`, do not share its state.
- The toggle, the shape pair and the size strip are `#phoneTools`, shown under the Layout row; hidden in Camera only (there is no screen), which suspends the phone version until a layout with a screen is chosen again. In again mode the stored phone settings preselect it.
- `ensureExport` renders the phone file when the toggle is on (`S.mobile = {blob, width, height, region, variant}`); changing the region, shape, layout, trim or cuts invalidates it like the landscape export. A failed phone render keeps the landscape upload working: the message says "Could not render the phone version (...). Upload and Download MP4 go on without it." and the toggle shows struck, as the camera toggle does when refused.
- The Details panel adds the phone version's size and render speed.

## Upload and storage (`public/upload.js`, `worker/index.js`, `schema.sql`, `migrations/0003-mobile.sql`)

- `POST /api/videos` and `POST /api/videos/:id/again` take `mobile: {size, width, height, region, variant} | null`, validated like `raw` (size against the cap; width and height integers; region shares 0 to 1 with w and h at least 0.1; variant wide or square). The row gains `mobile TEXT` (JSON `{size, uploadId, ready, width, height, region, variant}`) by `migrations/0003-mobile.sql` (one `ADD COLUMN`, applied by `scripts/migrate.mjs`; `schema.sql` gains the column with a comment). The file is a fourth track, `mobile`, on the existing blob, parts and complete routes (`?track=mobile`, key `videos/<id>-mobile.mp4`); the poster goes to `PUT /api/videos/:id/thumb?track=mobile` (key `thumbs/<id>-mobile.jpg`). `TRACKS` gains `mobile`; `RAW` stays the two originals.
- Upload order: the landscape video, the thumbnail, the phone file and its poster (so the link works first), then the originals. A phone upload that fails leaves the link working with "Phone version not kept" and a Retry that sends only what is missing, like the originals.
- Public routes: `GET /media/:id?mobile` (or `/media/:id/mobile`, builder's call, documented) and `GET /thumb/:id?mobile` serve the phone file and poster when `mobile.ready`; 404 otherwise. Range support as for the landscape file. The share page adds `?v=` to those addresses too. Again mode: a pending version carries `mobile`; a new phone file replaces the old on complete; a version without one removes the old file and clears the column.
- `DELETE` removes the two new objects and aborts an open phone upload; `GET /api/videos` and `GET /api/videos/:id` include `mobile` (ready or not).
- Security: the new track and keys are allow-listed; nothing from the request reaches a key; the public routes serve only ready files. The Worker diff gets a fresh security review.

## Share page (`worker/share-page.js`, `public/share.css`, `public/share.js` if a script is needed beyond the existing one)

The page knows both files (sizes and dimensions) and decides which `<video>` source to use before the first paint; the switch swaps `src`, `poster`, `width` and `height` and keeps the current time. The nonce CSP stays; any new script goes in the same nonce block. Download links for both.

## Decided during the build (deviations from the text above)

Recorded by the builder as they happen, so the spec and the code agree; the CHANGELOG carries the user-facing versions.

- Files: besides `public/region.js`, the phone version's part of the edit step (toggle, the box's place over the picture, the plate and poster, the second render, its upload after the link) is `public/phone.js`, and keeping the originals moved from `editor.js` to `public/keep.js`, so `editor.js` stays under 500 lines (483). The Worker's checks of what an upload declares (video fields, bubble, layout, `mobile`) moved to `worker/fields.js`, so `worker/index.js` stays under 500 (486).
- Public routes: the path form, `/media/<id>/mobile` and `/thumb/<id>/mobile` (with `?v=` from the phone file's size on the share page). They read the `mobile` column only for these addresses, so a database not yet updated keeps serving the landscape file.
- The camera follows the layout: the phone version shows it below the rule when the layout shows it (Bubble, Split); Screen only and Padded leave it out (black below, or the padded colour in Padded), even with a camera recorded. Camera only hides the toggle and suspends the phone version (fix round 1; it was forced off before): nothing is rendered or uploaded for it there, and a layout with a screen brings it back as it was, still on.
- Sizes are shares of the screen's width, but a box too tall for the screen at that width (Square on a 16:9 screen at Two thirds or Whole) is made as large as fits, so Square Whole is the screen's height. The upload stores the box itself (`region: {x, y, w, h}`, x and y its top left corner) with `variant`; again mode finds the size whose box is nearest that width. The region is not saved in the browser between recordings: each new recording starts centred, two thirds, wide.
- While the box shows, the preview keeps its composed form even in Screen only (picture on top, the player's controls on their strip, Full screen as the square at the row's end), so the box sits exactly on the picture and never over the controls.
- The box: the 3px vermilion outline is drawn inside its edge; the dimming covers the rest of the screen picture only (a box-shadow clipped to an area over the picture), so the camera's column in Split is not dimmed. It thickens to 6px on hover, keyboard focus and drag (the bubble's ring would be clipped at the picture's edge), sits under the bubble, and keeps its 3px outline when held (inert).
- The phone file is keyed on what decides it: the kept ranges, the box, the shape, and, from the layout, only whether the camera shows and the padded colour. Changing the layout from Bubble to Split, say, keeps it; the spec's other changes all re-render it.
- A failed phone render unchecks the toggle (the struck plate) and says "Could not render the phone version (...). Upload and Download MP4 go on without it."; turning it on again tries again.
- The poster is a 540x960 JPEG, drawn by `grabLayout` with the screen decoded wider so the region stays sharp; the plate refreshes 300 ms after a change, at the thumbnail's frame.
- Messages: "Sending the phone version: x of y MB...", then "Phone version kept: phones that open the link play it." or "Phone version not kept: the vertical file was not stored (...). The link works without it. Retry sends only what is missing." The message sits under Upload's until the link is made, then moves into the link box above the originals' note (the phone file goes up first). Download phone MP4 moves with Download MP4 into the link's row.
- Upload: a first upload's phone file goes single-shot or in parts by size, like the others; a version rendered again opens its phone upload as multipart, so R2 replaces the old file only when it completes. The video completes first (completing the phone version while the video of that version is still uploading is 409). When the video completes, the row takes the new phone record (not ready), so until the new phone file completes the share page offers the landscape file alone; a version without one removes the old phone file and poster and clears the column. An unfinished older phone upload is aborted then, and by a stale again being replaced, and by DELETE.
- `GET /api/videos` and `GET /api/videos/:id` show `mobile` as `{size, ready, width, height, region, variant}`, never its upload id. The library shows "Phone version" only once the file is complete.
- Share page: the choice is made by a second nonce script right after the player (same nonce as the page's script), so the player gets its source before the first paint; the switch is wired in the existing script. "Taller than wide" is CSS `orientation: portrait`, so a square viewport counts as portrait. The choice is stored (sessionStorage `vignette.view.<id>`) only when the viewer switches by hand. Without JavaScript the landscape player in a `<noscript>` shows. The facts line shows the size of the file playing. The downloads read "Download" and "Download phone version"; on phones the second takes its own row.
- Naming (fix round 1, a call David can reverse): the region's shape pair is labelled "Kept part", not "Shape", so it does not read as the bubble's Shape pair in the row above (both have a Square). The glyphs, the radios' names and the segment titles (Wide, Square) are unchanged.
- The note under the tools (fix round 1): while the box shows and nothing is held, it reads "Drag the red box to choose the part of the screen the phone version keeps.", plus " Drag the bubble to place your webcam." in Bubble; while rendering or uploading, and once linked, the held-still copy is unchanged. `#regionHelp` stays as the box's screen-reader description. In the tool rows each label and its strip wrap as one unit (`.tools-pair`), 24px between pairs.
- Phone completion race (fix round 1, from the security review): completing the phone file updates the row only while its `mobile` column is still the record that was read (`AND mobile = ?`), and answers 409 otherwise, so a first upload's phone completion racing a Change layout's video completion cannot mark the new version's record ready. No test stages this race through the API; it is guarded by the statement alone.
- Glyphs: `i-phone` (the toggle, the plate, Download phone MP4, the share page's Phone), `i-reg-wide` and `i-reg-square` (the Kept part pair; Wide is also the share page's Full), `i-reg-half`, `i-reg-twothirds`, `i-reg-whole` (the size strip: the screen's frame with a filled box of that width).
- Render speed, measured on an Apple M1 Max with a 20 s recording (1080p screen, 720p camera), Bubble plus the phone version (Wide, two thirds), two runs each: in Chromium's full headless mode drawing on the GPU (ANGLE Metal), Bubble 10.4 and 10.6, the phone version 10.5 and 10.6 seconds of video per second; in the test suite's headless shell, which draws in software (SwiftShader), Bubble 7.3 and 7.5, the phone version 3.5 (its larger frame costs more to draw there). Details shows the phone version's line beside the layout's.
- Tests: the fake screen's corner squares ramp in brightness and briefly match the background after each wrap, so the builder's test counts a corner as shown when its median brightness differs from the band's plain background by more than 8 or it ramps by more than 20 across, and asks for the top left corner in at least two of five samples (fix round 1: one run saw it in only two of three); the absent corners must be absent in all. That check uses a Square Half region at the top left, with each corner box the middle 70% of where the screen's square would land: a Wide Half region there ends at the screen's centre, where the fake screen draws a disc about 270 px across, and under background throttling a full run read that disc as the bottom right square in every sample.
